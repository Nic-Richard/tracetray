## Full description

Understand how people use your website with TraceTray.

Sign in to your existing TraceTray account to check your websites from your phone.
See visits, engagement and goal progress, then look closer at the pages and journeys
behind those numbers.

• Check traffic and behaviour trends across desktop and phone visits.
• Explore groups of visitors who browse in similar ways.
• View attention pictures with click and scroll measurements.
• Follow journeys between pages and see where expected paths lose visitors.
• Create and manage goals for clicks, destination pages and form submissions.
• Compare behaviour before and after a website change.
• Ask questions about your website's collected analytics.
• Manage websites, email preferences and account settings.

Available features and usage limits depend on your TraceTray account's plan.
Tracking code must already be installed on your website to collect visit data.
Automated explanations can be incomplete or incorrect; use them alongside the measurements.

The Android app provides access to your existing TraceTray service. It does not sell
subscriptions or upgrades inside the app.
