What TraceTray collects

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Everything the TraceTray tracker sends from your visitors' browsers, what it never touches, and how long it's kept. Written for site owners deciding whether TraceTray fits their privacy policy.

What it never collects

  • Anything typed: no keystrokes, and nothing entered into forms.
  • What's on the page: no screenshots, video, recordings or copies of page content.
  • Query strings, so search terms, email addresses or tokens in a page's address aren't sent.
  • IP addresses: the server uses one only for a moment (see below) and never writes it down, including in its access logs.
  • Cookies, or anything else stored on the visitor's device.
  • Names, email addresses, account details or anything that identifies a visitor from one day to the next.

How visits are grouped without cookies

To see journeys across pages, TraceTray needs to know that two page views came from the same visit. Instead of storing an ID in the browser, the server mixes a random value with your site's key, the visitor's IP address and browser details, and keeps only the scrambled result.

The random value changes every day and the old one is deleted, so the same person gets a new, unrelated ID each day and IDs can't be traced back to an IP address. A journey ends after 30 minutes without activity. The IP address is also used, in memory only, to limit how many requests one address can send in a minute.

Where it's kept, and for how long

  • Detailed movement data (cursor positions, clicks, taps and scrolling) is deleted after 90 days.
  • Each visit's summary (page, duration, device and counts) and your analysis results are kept for as long as your account exists. How much history the dashboard shows depends on your plan.
  • After a subscription ends, your website data is kept for 30 days, with a notice 7 days before it's deleted.
  • Encrypted database backups are kept for 30 days.
  • Data is processed in the United States, on TraceTray's server and with the providers listed in the privacy policy. It isn't sold, shared or used for ads.

For your own privacy policy

Your visitors should know that you use behavioural analytics. A sample paragraph you can adapt:

We use TraceTray to understand how visitors use this website. It records cursor movement, pauses, clicks, taps and scrolling on our pages, without cookies and without storing anything on your device. It doesn't record what you type, the content of our pages, or your IP address. Page views from one visit are grouped with an identifier that changes every day. TraceTray processes this data for us in the United States.

Whether you also need consent depends on your other tools and where your visitors are, so check with your own adviser. For a data processing agreement or other questions, write to support@tracetray.com.
