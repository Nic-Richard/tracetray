2. What TraceTray is

TraceTray is a behavioural web analytics service. It records selected cursor, touch, click, scroll, pause, page, and session events, then calculates patterns intended to help website owners understand how visitors browse their pages.

TraceTray is still developing. Features, analysis methods, stored fields, and availability may change as the service develops. Changes to what a plan costs are covered in section 4.

4. Plans and billing

TraceTray offers Starter, Pro and Ultra plans, billed monthly or yearly. Each starts with a 30-day free trial, one per account. A payment card is required to start it. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your card is charged for the plan you chose, and the subscription renews automatically each month or year until you cancel. You will get a reminder before the trial ends.

You can cancel at any time from the billing portal. Cancelling stops future charges, and the plan stays active until the end of the period already paid for. Payments are not refunded except where the law requires it. Prices are shown in US dollars before you subscribe; if a price changes, current subscribers are told before the new price applies to them.

Each plan has limits on websites, pages tracked, monthly visits, history and Ask questions, and some features are included only in certain plans, as shown on the pricing page. Features marked as coming soon are not part of a plan until they are released. Going over a limit does not stop tracking: visits past the monthly allowance keep only their summary, and pages past the limit are locked until the next month or an upgrade.

After a subscription ends, TraceTray keeps the account's website data for 30 days and sends a notice 7 days before deleting it. The website data is then deleted; the account itself stays so it can be used again.
