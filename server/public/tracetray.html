Who is reading and who is scanning

See the few kinds of visitor each page gets, such as people who decide fast, people who compare and people who skim, and how many of each.

Every visit becomes a profile of 13 behavioural measures on desktop and 8 on phones: cursor speed, pause rate, scroll depth, hesitation before clicks, how widely attention ranges and more. Machine learning clusters similar profiles into groups.

  • The number of groups is chosen by how cleanly the data separates, not fixed in advance.
  • Results are cross-checked against a second clustering method.
  • Statistical tests confirm which differences between groups are real.

What to change, and why

Every visitor group comes with a clear next step, like “keep the path short for people who already know what they want.” Each one is based on how that group actually behaves and what the page is for, so you know which fix will do the most for conversions.

Every page also gets a summary of what stands out, and groups keep the same names between analyses, so changes are easy to track.

Questions

How is this different from other analytics?

Pageview tools count visits. Heatmaps and recordings show where people clicked. TraceTray compares every visit, groups visitors by behaviour and tells you what each group needs, with no proxy or server setup to keep data flowing.

What does the tracker collect?

Cursor movement, pauses, clicks, taps, scrolling, page depth, timing and the text of clicked elements. Never keystrokes, form values, screenshots or video. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on the visitor's device; visits are grouped with an identifier that changes daily and never stores the IP address.

Do I need a cookie banner for TraceTray?

TraceTray sets no cookies and stores nothing on visitors' devices, which is what EU and UK cookie rules cover. Whether your site needs consent overall depends on your other tools and where your visitors are, so check with your own adviser.

Who is it for?

Site owners, marketers, UX designers and product teams who want to improve user experience and conversions: stores, SaaS sites, portfolios and blogs, including teams that can't use recording tools, like health, finance, legal and education sites. No analytics background needed.

How does the free trial work?

Every plan starts with 30 days free, once per account. Cancel anytime during the trial and you won't be charged. Compare plans to see what each one includes.

How many visits do I need?

Fewer than you'd think. Groups appear once a page has a modest number of active visits, and each analysis shows how many it used.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Taps, scrolling and reading pauses are tracked on phones, and mobile is analyzed separately.

Can I track a whole site?

Yes. Add the snippet site-wide and group pages that share a URL pattern.
